Privacy

Privacy Policy

Phosphors CRT · Last updated 25 June 2026

Phosphors does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Phosphors is a local video-processing app. Everything it does, opening your clips, applying the CRT simulation, and recording the result, happens entirely on your Mac. The app has no network access and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Your files

The video files you open and the recordings you create stay on your device, under your control. Phosphors never uploads or shares them.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Phosphors does not see or store any payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Phosphors collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log standard server request data as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
